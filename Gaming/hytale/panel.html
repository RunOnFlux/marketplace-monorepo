About Flux Deployment

Important information for your server

Persistent Storage (g:/ mode)

Your /data directory persists across container restarts. This includes auth tokens, server files, and world saves. On g:/ deployments, data syncs across Flux nodes.

Panel Security

This panel is accessible via your app URL. Ensure you've configured a strong HYTALE_PANEL_PASSWORD in your Flux app environment variables.
